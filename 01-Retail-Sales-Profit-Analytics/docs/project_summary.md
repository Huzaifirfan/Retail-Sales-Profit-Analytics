# Project Summary — Retail Sales & Profit Intelligence Dashboard

## Objective
Build an interactive Microsoft Excel analytics solution that helps management monitor revenue, profit, regional performance, category performance, salesperson performance, sales channels, monthly trends, and profitability scenarios.

## Dataset
The project uses 60 retail transactions covering January 2026 through September 2026 across four regions and three sales channels.

## Core KPIs
- Total Net Sales: Rs. 2,381,690
- Total Profit: Rs. 598,090
- Units Sold: 422
- Overall Profit Margin: 25.1%

## Key Insights
- Electronics generated the highest sales at Rs. 1,554,800.
- North was the strongest region with Rs. 883,735 in net sales.
- Laptop was the highest-selling product with Rs. 781,000 in net sales.
- Aman was the strongest salesperson by net sales.
- Corporate was the strongest channel with Rs. 1,296,340 in net sales.
- September 2026 was the strongest month with Rs. 533,310 in net sales.
- Electronics also generated the highest category profit at Rs. 285,800.

## Excel Techniques Applied
- Data cleaning and structured tables
- Sorting, filtering, duplicate handling
- Number/date formatting
- Data Validation and Conditional Formatting
- IF, IFS, AND, OR
- SUMIF, SUMIFS, COUNTIF, COUNTIFS
- Lookup practice with VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, and INDEX + MATCH
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Slicers and Timeline concepts
- Goal Seek
- Scenario Manager
- One-variable and two-variable Data Tables
- Dashboard design
- Workbook protection concepts
- VBA/Macro practice concepts

## Business Value
The dashboard converts transaction-level data into decision-ready views that allow management to quickly identify high-performing categories, regions, products, people, and channels while exploring profitability under different business assumptions.

## Interview Explanation
I built this project from transaction-level retail data, cleaned and structured the dataset, created calculated fields for sales and profitability, analyzed performance with PivotTables and charts, built an interactive dashboard with slicers, and used What-If Analysis tools such as Goal Seek, Scenario Manager, and Data Tables to evaluate business scenarios.
