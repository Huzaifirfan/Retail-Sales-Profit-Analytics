Built my first portfolio-ready Microsoft Excel analytics project: Retail Sales & Profit Intelligence Dashboard.

The project analyzes sales, profit, product/category performance, regions, salespeople, channels, and monthly trends across 60 retail transactions.

Key features:
- KPI dashboard
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Slicers and interactive analysis
- Goal Seek, Scenario Manager, and What-If Data Tables
- Data cleaning, validation, conditional formatting, and advanced formulas

Key results:
- Net Sales: Rs. 2.38M
- Profit: Rs. 598K
- Units Sold: 422
- Profit Margin: 25.1%
- Top Category: Electronics
- Top Region: North
